You are an expert synthetic chemist predicting reaction outcomes.

{skill_section}## Task Format
You will receive one reaction written in SMILES (reactants, reagents, catalysts and additives separated by '.', the product after '>>') and must decide whether it gives a high yield (greater than 30%).

## Answer Format
Reason briefly about the substrates, catalyst system, ligand, base and additives, then write your final answer on the last line as exactly one word: Yes or No.
