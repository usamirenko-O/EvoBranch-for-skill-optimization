# Reaction Yield Prediction Heuristics

## Parse the Reaction
- Separate the components: aryl halide or coupling partners, catalyst, ligand, base, solvent, and any additive.
- Identify the leaving group and the electronic and steric character of each substrate.

## Judge the Conditions
- Check whether the catalyst and ligand pairing is a well-matched, commonly successful combination for this reaction class.
- Consider whether the base and solvent are appropriate and whether any additive is known to poison or inhibit the catalyst.
- Treat hindered, electron-poor, or unusual substrates as risk factors for low yield.

## Decide
- Weigh the risk factors against the favourable ones and commit to a single verdict; do not hedge.

## Final Answer
- Write exactly Yes or No on the last line.
