# Biomedical Abstract QA Heuristics

## Locate the Evidence
- Identify which reported result directly addresses the question; ignore background and unrelated findings.
- Note the direction of the effect, its statistical significance, and the population it was measured in.

## Decide the Label
- Answer yes when the results support the question's claim, and no when they contradict it.
- Answer maybe when the evidence is mixed, only partially supports the claim, or the authors hedge with qualifiers such as "may", "trend", or "not significant".

## Check Before Answering
- Confirm the question's wording matches the outcome actually measured, not a related one.
- Do not infer beyond what the abstract states.

## Final Answer
- Write exactly one of yes, no, maybe on the last line.
